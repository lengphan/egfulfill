Order in, parcel out

How EGFULFILL works

Five steps from a marketplace sale to a tracked parcel. You approve the artwork — the vetted print network runs everything else, completely hands-off.

Step 01 · Sync

Every order lands in one queue

Etsy, Shopify and TikTok orders normalise into a single schema the moment they're placed — no CSV exports, no tab-hopping.

Step 02 · Design

Approve the artwork

Mockups, embroidery digitising and automatic thread-matching sit right where the order lives — sign off without leaving the queue.

Step 03 · Print

Printed on a vetted network

DTG or embroidery on quality-scored presses. What ships matches the mockup you approved — every single run.

Step 04 · Check

Inspected against spec

Every piece is quality-checked and packed to spec before it leaves the floor — flagged back to you if anything looks off.

Step 05 · Ship

Tracked to the door

The cheapest label is bought automatically and tracking is pushed straight back to the marketplace and your buyer.

Questions, answered

Everything you'd ask first

Hover a question to open it — no clicking.

Do I need to hold my own inventory?
No. Blanks are stocked across the vetted print network — you only pay for a garment when a customer actually orders it, so there's nothing to buy up front or warehouse.
Which channels can I connect?
Etsy, Shopify, TikTok Shop, Amazon, WooCommerce and more. Every order drops into one shared queue the second it's placed — no tab-hopping between dashboards.
How does billing work?
A prepaid wallet auto-charges per order — blank, print and label itemised on every line. No monthly minimums and no surprise invoice at the end of the month.
Can I approve artwork before it prints?
Always. Every order shows a mockup on the real garment with thread colours matched to stock — nothing goes to the press until you've signed it off.
Can my team help run it?
Yes — invite staff with scoped access (orders, design, or full) on one shared wallet, so the right people can move work without seeing everything.
