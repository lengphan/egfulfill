# Designer — How-to Guide

You work **one board**: the artwork/digitizing queue. You never handle orders, stock, or money — and you **never see which seller** a job belongs to. That privacy is deliberate. After sign-in you land on the **Board**.

---

## Work a card

1. Open **Board**. Each card is a job needing artwork or digitizing.

   ![Designer board](../images/designer-board.png)

2. **Claim** a card to take it — your name shows as the claimer (designers appear in violet, factory in slate, the Pink Design partner in pink).
3. Click the card to open the full brief: the artwork, the description/notes, and any **reference files** (they stay attached to the card).
4. Do the design work.
5. **Send for review**. An approver (admin) moves it to the **approved** lane. Note: a file *existing* isn't the same as *approved* — the chip stays amber until it's approved, then turns violet.

### Need embroidery digitizing?

Use the **Digitizer**: drop the artwork and any text, arrange the layers on the canvas (drag to move, corners to resize, top nub to rotate, drag cards to reorder), then export the **machine file (.emb)** or a **PNG** preview.

![Digitizer](../images/digitizer.png)

## Shared

**Chat** (reach support), **Help**, **Notifications** (updates on your cards), **Settings** (your profile).

---

## Not yours

- **Orders, Shipping, Inventory, Purchasing, Finance** — a designer works artwork only; these aren't on your board.
- You can't buy design files or see any seller's balance.
- **Cross-seller privacy is absolute:** never learn or reveal that a design was used by another seller. If you spot a duplicate, it's a factory-only concern — don't act on it.
